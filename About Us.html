About Us

Gen Fitness was started because of my belief that everyone in our community deserves to enjoy a healthy lifestyle without having to worry about the costs or the barriers that often get in the way when people want to start their fitness journeys. Many people want to feel safe and have a fitness community to be able to motivate each other and that is what we strive to provide at Gen Fitness. Our classes are designed for all ages and fitness levels, so don’t worry about having the perfect form for workouts and start your fitness journey with us today.
